# Favorites: Back Up & Restore

Lemuel 2026-10-21: users find Settings → Import/Export Faves confusing. Tried it on the emulator:
**Import crashed the app when the clipboard was empty** (`NullPointerException` in `FavoriteSettingsDialog`); it needed
copy-paste through the clipboard; the exported text (`FR216,E1,`) meant nothing to a person; Export used the old
"Just once / Always" picker; the order was lost (exported from a `HashSet`, imported with one timestamp); and it was
hidden in Settings under technical words. Replaced (`FavoriteSettingsDialog` removed):

- **Favorites tab:** a bar at the top, "6 favorites" (or how to add one) with **Back up** and **Restore**. The tab now lists
  all favorites (it stopped at 100) and no longer pops the keyboard up (it cannot be searched).
- **Back up:** a readable text, a short note plus one "E1 – first line" per hymn, newest first. "Send" gives the system share
  sheet with `hymns-favorites.txt` attached and the text as the body; "Save as a file" uses the system file picker.
- **Restore:** open the file (picker), paste the text, or **tap the file in Gmail / Drive / Files**: the app shows up as
  "Hymns – Restore favorites" (`RestoreFavoritesActivity`, `VIEW text/plain`). It reads the first word of each line, so
  email quoting and "Sent from my iPhone" don't matter, and still reads the old `E1,C215,` lists. It asks "Add 3 hymns to your
  favorites?" and says how many are already there or not in this version, and keeps the order.
- **Settings:** "Back Up & Restore Favorites — Keep a copy, or move them to a new phone" opens a dialog with both, and says that a
  new phone set up from the Google backup usually brings them along.
- **Android's own backup:** checked with `bmgr` (backup, `pm clear`, restore): the favorites and history come back. It was also
  backing up the 18 MB hymn database, and Google keeps at most 25 MB per app (a bigger backup fails as a whole, favorites
  included), so `hymns.sqlite` is now excluded (`res/xml/backup_rules.xml`, `data_extraction_rules.xml`); the app copies it
  from its assets on first launch, checked.
- **Web app:** reads the same text (same first-word rule) and exports it; hint updated; e2e test added (57/57 pass).
- Tested on the emulator: bar, share sheet, save to Downloads (file content checked), restore from an email-style file and
  an old-format file (through the Files app's "Open with"), empty clipboard (message, no crash), Settings dialog.
- **Second round (same day):** unit tests added (`FavoritesBackupTest`, `LogBookTest` with Robolectric; 20 pass). They found that
  `addAll` could put a restored hymn below a favorite added a few milliseconds before; fixed (restored hymns start above the newest).
  On the emulator: paste from the clipboard, Cancel, un-hearting updates the count, rotation, empty favorites, restore from
  Settings, system font at 150%, Night Mode (the search screen stays light, as before). Three fixes from that:
  - Android offers "Open with Hymns" for **every** text file (with "Always"). A file that is not a backup now says so and has
    **Open with another app** (a chooser without Hymns); the restore screen stays until an app is picked, as the permission to

# French Lyrics Under the Sheet Music

Request from a brother in France. A French hymn borrows its English hymn's sheet, so the notes showed English words.
Decided 2026-10-17: all 248 French hymns, French shown automatically when the sheet is opened from a French hymn,
syllables kept in a small file per hymn (not a second copy of the sheet). Work is in `tools/sheet-lyrics/` (README there) and
on branch `french-sheet-lyrics`; not merged yet.

- **How:** `tools/sheet-lyrics/french.py` gives each French word its possible sung readings (silent -e sung or not, "ion" as one or two
  syllables, "qu"/"gu" silent u, elisions...). `align.py` counts the note slots of each English phrase from the `.mei`, then
  picks one reading per word so the French fills exactly those notes. Output: `app/src/main/assets/sheetLyrics/FR<n>.json`
  (syllable per note id, French title, verses 2+). The viewer swaps them in (`applyLyrics` in `viewer.js`, `?lyrics=FR<n>`);
  `SheetMusicButton` passes the hymn being read. Verses 2+ are the database stanzas as they are.
- **Result (239 of the 248 French hymns have a sheet to borrow):** 188 fit exactly, **34 adapted** (a syllable held over two notes, or two weak
  syllables sharing a note, like a choir does; never a changed word; viewer draws held syllables with an extender line),
  2 fit but the French lines do not end where the English phrases end (FR20, FR74), **15 still need a person** (the French
  text is much shorter or longer than the music, or the music repeats words the French stanza lacks): FR25, 41, 54, 75, 76,
  105, 110, 156, 160, 168, 188, 235, 243, 247, 248. 9 French hymns have no sheet at all (FR53, 60, 65, 91, 101, 129,
  175, 176, 182). Hymns still waiting keep the English sheet. `tools/sheet-lyrics/review/INDEX.md` lists everything the brother in
  France should look at: the adapted ones (each has a file showing the result, `_` = held, `‿` = shared note) and the 18.
- Tested on the emulator: FR1, FR10, FR216 (piano and guitar); English sheets and hymns without an overlay are unchanged.
- Left out on purpose: the subtitle on French sheets (the database has the French categories in capitals without accents).
- Data typos seen in the French stanzas ("L' Esprit", "L 'Esprit", "Qu' Il"): the tool ignores them, but they show in the app.
- [ ] Send `tools/sheet-lyrics/review/` to the brother in France to check the adapted hymns and fill the 18; merge when you are happy; not released yet.

# Chinese Translations of New Songs

## The problem

hymnal.net has Chinese versions of some New Songs at `ns/<n>c` (traditional) and `ns/<n>c?gb=1` (simplified), e.g.
`ns/881c`. Our database doesn't have them, but 18 hymns already link to them as `C<n>c`, because the hymnal.net download
records the link:

NS481, NS506, NS510, NS513, NS517, NS618, NS654, NS680, NS881, NS888, NS923, NS1031, NS1040, NS1104, NS1141, NS1142,
SY104, SY164.

Effects today:
- "Related:" shows an ID like `C881c` that leads nowhere (display text only, not tappable).
- Choosing 中文-繁 in the language menu on one of these hymns looks up `C881c`, doesn't find it, and lands on C1.

There are too few of them for a group of their own.

## Proposed approach (not decided)

Put them in the existing Chinese groups under hymnal.net's own number: `C881c` in C (traditional) and `Z881c` in Z
(simplified).
- The 18 existing links start working with no other change, and the language menu lands on the right hymn.
- The `c` keeps them apart from the hymnal's own C881 (a different hymn). No new group or icon.

Check first:
- They would be the first IDs with a letter in the number. See where they sort in the C/Z lists (ideally right after
  C881) and that search by number still works.
- `HymnGroup.getHymnNoFromID` / `getHymnGroupFromID` with `C881c`, and the parent/related logic in `HymnsDao`.

Alternative: don't add them, and hide related links to hymns that aren't in the database. Simpler, but the links stay dead.
