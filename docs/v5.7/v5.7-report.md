**Decided (can be changed):** group codes `R`, `MY`, `TA` (IDs have at most two letters; `R` is also what the German
file uses for this Russian book). Drawer names in English like most books: Russian, Burmese, Tamil; the web app shows
Русский, မြန်မာ, தமிழ். Colours not used by another book: dark blue, dark green, blue grey (pink was too close to
French). Placeholder icons `r.png`, `my.png`, `ta.png`.

**How:** one script for this file format, `ProvisionLanguageFile.groovy`, with a settings table per language; Gradle
`runRussian`, `runBurmese`, `runTamil`. Each task resets the database first (`importSql`), so run the three in one call:
`./gradlew :databaseProvisioner:runRussian :databaseProvisioner:runBurmese :databaseProvisioner:runTamil :sqlite:exportSql`.
Only a bare number starts a stanza, so an end-note listing "1. Куплеты 1-8 и 26" stays in the note.

  chorus, 3, chorus): R185, R210, R212, R300, R309, R361, R363, R388, R431, R433, R434, R450, R509, R516, R595, R597.

# Normalize Authors and Composers

Lemuel 2026-10-25 (after E1358's author became "Adapted from Fanny Jane Crosby (1820-1915)", as on hymnal.net): make
author and composer names consistent. Today the same person is written several ways (863 different authors, 1,031
composers), e.g.:
- Fanny Jane Crosby (1820-1915) ×22, Fanny Jane Crosby ×14, Fanny J. Crosby ×4, "Fanny Jane Crosby bio" ×1
- Ira David Sankey (1840-1908) ×38, Ira David Sankey ×22, I. D. Sankey ×5, J. D. Sankey ×5 (a typo), Ira D. Sankey ×1
- William Howard Doane ×28, William Howard Doane (1832-1915) ×27, W. H. Doane ×3

Proposed: hymnal.net's form (full name and years, "Adapted from …" where it says so). Fix the stray "bio" (a link text
copied from hymnal.net). The Authors browsing in the v5.8 search lists by name, so this makes it much more useful.

**`*` and `+` (checked 2026-10-25):** 243 English hymns have the author `*` and 28 have `+` (none in composer). They
are the printed hymnal's marks: `*` = Witness Lee, `+` = Watchman Nee. hymnal.net shows the names (checked E10, E86,
E200, E622, E782, E848, E914: "Witness Lee (1905-1997)"; E279, E412, E475, E635, E873: "Watchman Nee (1903-1972)";
E1003: "Augustus Montague Toplady (1740-1778); Adapted by Watchman Nee (1903-1972)", so take each hymn's text from
hymnal.net, not one replacement). Today the hymn page shows "Author: *" (and so do the 3,201 translations that inherit it from their English parent),
and the Authors tab shows "* LSM" and "+ Translated" (`AuthorAdapter.retouch`; "Translated" is wrong).

# Hymn Page: Other Languages at the Top

A brother complained (2026-10-25) about v5.6 moving the related hymns to the bottom: in a meeting in France a French
hymn was called, and the English and Chinese speakers needed their numbers; now the brothers had to scroll past every
stanza to find them. Designs compared in a mockup (https://claude.ai/artifact/7PfJYHaGv1cpQc9v1rDVT4): Lemuel chose
the **number grid**, with abbreviations instead of book names, no colour dots, wrapping (no sideways scroll), and a
**Hide** that folds it into one row and is remembered.

Done on master 2026-10-25:
- **Under the subject:** a row `OTHER LANGUAGES 18  Hide ▴` and a grid of code + number (`CS 305`, `FR 115`), 4
  columns on most phones (2–8 depending on width and font size), the codes one width so the numbers line up. Each
  number opens that hymn.
- **Drawer order** (the `HymnGroup` order, then the number), not the order the database happens to store: English and
  Chinese always come first.
- **Hide / Show** folds the grid into the row; one setting for every hymn (`otherLanguagesFolded`), kept across
  restarts, and the pages next to the current one follow at once. New installs start open. Hide/Show is in the book's
  colour.
- **Details** (Meter, Time/Key, Tune, Verses, Author, Composer) moved to a card at the bottom, a little smaller than the
  lyrics, as a two-column table (label | value, the value wrapping in its own column; Lemuel's idea, like the mockup). Folded, stanza 1 starts higher than in v5.6; open, at about the same place.
- The header card now always shows (it holds the buttons); before, a hymn with no subject and no details lost its
  buttons.
- Code: `OtherLanguagesSection` (rewritten), `LyricsArea`, `content_area.xml` / `content_area_night.xml`. Docs:
  `HYMNAL_SPEC.md` §6.1 and §10.
- Checked on the emulator (Pixel 9 Pro XL): E1159 (18 languages), fold, the next hymns built beside it (E1160 with
  none: no row; E1161: folded), app restart (still folded), a tap on FR 115 (opens it; E 1159 first in its grid),
  night mode. Unit tests pass.
- Explored on the emulator by a test agent (2026-10-25, ~50 min): no crash; grid, fold, Back, landscape, large font,
  night mode, Farsi, Russian/Burmese/Tamil, search, buttons, sheet music and Settings all fine. Found: the button pill
  grew when a hymn was favorited (the filled heart was an old 36dp PNG in `drawable-xhdpi` that won over the 28dp
  vector of the same name; PNG removed, now the same size). Also seen: the page looked ~7% grey after an adb swipe
  until a tap, probably the emulator's screen dimming; to check on a real phone.
- Not checked: tablet, XXL font in depth.
- Web app: shows the other languages at the top already (one row that scrolls sideways on phones); it could get the

# Lyrics That Differ From hymnal.net

Found 2026-10-25 by the authors fetch (`Normalize Authors and Composers`): it checks that each hymnal.net page is the
right hymn by comparing first lines, and 5 of the E/NS pages didn't match ours. All 5 are the right page; the
difference was in our data.

Fixed 2026-10-25 (compared line by line with hymnal.net; only words and punctuation, not quote/dash style):
- **E187**: its first stanza line and first chorus line were taken from the end-note ("Verses 1 to 8 and 26", "THIS
  HYMN MAY BE USED AS FIVE SEPARATE"), so First Lines showed the note; it has no chorus. Stanza 23 lacked a word
  ("And we in Thee are" → "…are branches;"), plus "fulness" → "fullness", a stray comma ("The truth that, brings"),
  "life divine," → ".", "in spirit," → ";".
- **E188**: same as E187, its first chorus line was the end-note ("THIS HYMN MAY BE SHORTENED…"); it has no chorus.
- **E1071**: "Sinner Jesus will receive" → "Sinners…".
- **E1173**: "Grant us a vision" → "Grant us the vision".
- **E1215**: "God had not giv'n us" → "God has not…" (2 Tim. 1:7). Its composer "Lewis Ellis Jones" is "Lewis Edgar
  Jones" on hymnal.net: fixed with the authors.
- **MY556** (Burmese): stanza 1 began with the Bible reference "ဆာလံ၊၁၁၆း၁၂-၁၃", which is also its end-note, so its first
  line was the reference. Removed from stanza 1 (`Burmese.txt` too; send it back to the brother with the next file).

**New Songs revised on hymnal.net (open):** NS21 on hymnal.net is a revised translation ("Our hearts, Lord, now You
fully own, / We love You, Lord, and You alone…") of the one we have ("Lord Jesus, all our hearts You've gained, / You
are the only One we love…"); same song and choruses, better wording and rhyme. Our New Songs were downloaded years
