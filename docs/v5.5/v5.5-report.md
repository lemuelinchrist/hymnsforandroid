## Converting the `.docx` to text

The dev machine has no `pandoc`, `unzip` or `python-docx`, so the text was extracted with plain Python: `zipfile` plus `xml.etree` over `word/document.xml`.

**Gotcha: floating text boxes.** The document has **63 text boxes**. Word stores each one twice: a modern `mc:AlternateContent` choice and a legacy VML `mc:Fallback` copy. A simple regex over `<w:t>` elements therefore:
- **duplicates** every text-box line, and
- **merges** text-box paragraphs into the paragraph that anchors them (e.g. `Sikuabudu pekee yangu1. Nimetekwa...`).

Fix: walk the XML tree, skip everything under `mc:Fallback`, and emit each `w:txbxContent` paragraph as its own line. Treat `w:br` as a newline and `w:tab` as a tab. The result was 3,270 lines of clean text, with no duplicates.

Headers and footers contain only page numbers, so no hymn titles are hidden there.

## Problems found

1. **The first hymn has no title.** The document starts with `1. Kanisa la utukufu nasi tu washirika...`.
2. **Some titles aren't in caps**, so a caps-based detector misses them, e.g. `Sikuabudu pekee yangu`, `Wazo la 'kati' la Mungu-972`, `Damu, Neno,Roho na Kanisa`. These hymn boundaries need marking by hand.
3. **Some hymns aren't in Kiswahili.**
   - English: `I Will Sing the Wondrous Story` (#331), `Draw Me Nearer` (#387), `What Can Wash Away My Sin`, `Look Away! O Look Away` (#1206), "In tenderness He sought me", "Thy name is sweet as ointment poured forth".
   - Apparently Kikuyu: `NGIONA UTHERI`, and "Ndina gikeno ndiri na kieha".
4. **One section is badly broken by the text-box layout:** the English Song of Songs hymn "Thy name is sweet as ointment poured forth" (around lines 1560–1720 of the extracted text). It is split into fragments such as `SUS`, `ea-`, `lesis.`, with stanzas out of order.
5. **The layout is inconsistent.** Some stanzas run together on one line, while others are one line per line. Choruses have no label and are separated only by blank lines.
6. **There are probable typos**, e.g. `kalamnu` (karamu?), `niwili` (mwili?), `Halel'uyah sase` (sasa?). These are not safe to fix without a Kiswahili speaker.
7. **Some hymns have no English hymn number**, e.g. `Sikuabudu Pekee Yangu`, `Naangaziwa`, `Hakuna Kitu Kinachoshinda Damu`, `Utukufu kwa Baba`, `Nafsi Yangu Furahia`, `Tumeshinda`, `Nawacha Giza`, `Bwana Yesu Ndiye Njia` (sung to the tune of "Yes, Jesus Loves Me"), `Yesu Mwamba Wetu`, `Watoto wa Mungu`, `Tumeamua`, `Chakutumaini Sina`. # Tune Codes — Check Against hymnal.net (2026-10-07)

## Why

The SVG→LilyPond converter (`svg-to-lilypond/`) flagged 48 hymns whose database `tune` code does not match the melody on
their own sheet music. Decision (Lemuel): **follow hymnal.net as closely as possible**, since that is where the hymns come
from. So every hymn that has sheet music (3,179) was compared with the "Hymn Code" on its hymnal.net page.

Reminder: the tune code also names the MIDI file (`res/raw/m<code>.mid`, one file per code, shared by every hymn with that
code). Changing a code therefore means making sure `m<newcode>.mid` exists and is the right melody.

## How

- Page per group: `E`→`h/n`, `NS`→`ns/n` (`NS10000+k`→`lb/k`), `C`→`ch/n`, `CS`→`ts/n`, `CH`→`c/n`, `BF`→`nt/<n>`
  taken from `sheet_music_link` (`e0323_new` → `nt/323`). The code is read the same way `HymnalNetExtractor` does it
  (`label "Hymn Code"` → link text). One page per second, about an hour.
- Database side: `sqlite/hymns.sql` loaded into a scratch sqlite (never the app's `hymns.sqlite`).
- Each code was also compared with the melody read from our sheet (`svg-to-lilypond` IR, `verify.degrees`).
- Data and script: `docs/v5.5/tune-check/` (`hymnalnet_codes_2026-10-07.tsv` = every hymn; `differences_vs_sheet_2026-10-07.txt`
  = the 63 that are not plain matches; `fetch_hymnalnet_codes.py`; `empty_midi_files.txt`). The fetched HTML pages and
  downloaded MIDIs were in the session scratchpad and may be gone; the script refetches them.

# New Sheet Music Viewer (beta) (2026-10-09)

Built on branch `ly-viewer` and merged to master 2026-10-09 (fast-forward, no conflicts). The full design is in
`svg-to-lilypond/DESIGN.md` §19.

## What it is

- A setting, **New Sheet Music Viewer**, off by default. With it off, or for a hymn without a converted sheet, the app
  shows the SVG as before.
- The piano `.ly` files are converted to MEI (`svg-to-lilypond/tools/ly_to_mei.py`, all 3,179 convert) and drawn in the
  app by Verovio (LGPL-3.0, shipped unmodified with its notice). The sheet fits the screen width, and pinch to zoom lays
  the music out again instead of magnifying it.
- A tap shows or hides a bar: back, Piano | Guitar, transpose − / + (the guitar capo is worked out again from the new
  key), and Hide staff (chords over the lyrics, for guitar players). Everything resets when a sheet is opened again.
- The bar stays clear of the camera cutout, in portrait and landscape. Night mode works.
- Size: about 8.6 MB more in the APK (compressed). The SVG folders (about 615 MB raw) stay for now; once the viewer
  replaces them they will be removed, so there will be no fallback to the SVG.

## Checked

- All 3,179 MEI files load and render in Verovio; the chord view builds for all of them with every chord and syllable
  placed.
- Emulator (Pixel 9 Pro XL): E1, NS576, C103, E1016, BF185 in piano and guitar, transposed, staff on and off, night
  mode, a narrow screen (384 px), landscape, and the back button.
